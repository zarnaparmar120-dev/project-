Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a grooming session take?
It typically takes 1-2 hours depending on the breed, size of the pet, and the service package selected.
Do you handle aggressive or anxious dogs?
Yes, our staff is trained to handle anxious pets. We may schedule a longer appointment or a "meet and greet" first to ensure safety.
What products do you use?
We use only premium, hypoallergenic, and eco-friendly shampoos and conditioners that are safe for your pet's skin.
Should I vaccinate my pet before grooming?
Yes, we require up-to-date vaccinations (Rabies, Distemper) for all pets to ensure a safe environment for everyone.
Can I stay and watch?
Due to space constraints and safety reasons, we generally do not allow owners to stay in the grooming area, but you are welcome to wait in our lobby.
